## Baseline (Search Console, 26 August to 25 November 2026)

178 pages indexed, 172 not indexed. 708 clicks, 48.7K impressions, 1.5% click-through rate, average position 6.9. Compare 28 days after Phase 1 is deployed. No rise in indexed pages is promised.

## Findings behind Phase 1

- Sampled pages already declare a self-referencing canonical in `<head>`, so no canonical change is planned.
- Municipality pages are not near-empty: the sampled one has about 1,766 words and a unique summary.
- Sampled data files, share images and the feed send no `X-Robots-Tag`.
- The menu's "All statistics" points at `/dashboard`, which is noindex. `/series` is linked from the footer.
- Queries containing "population" had 1,854 impressions and 6 clicks. Municipality titles do not say "population".
