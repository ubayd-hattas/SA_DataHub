# Code of conduct

SA Data Hub is for anyone who wants to understand South Africa's numbers. Contributors and readers come from different places, languages and politics, and the project only works if everyone can take part without being attacked for that.

**Expected:** be respectful and assume good faith. Disagree about figures and methods with sources, not with insults. Say so when you are unsure.

**Not accepted:** harassment, personal attacks, slurs, abuse based on race, ethnicity, gender, sexuality, religion, disability, age, nationality or political views, publishing someone's private details, and using issues or pull requests to campaign for or against a party.

**What happens:** the maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, and block people who break these rules. To report a problem privately, email uhattas@gmail.com.

This applies in the repository's issues, pull requests and discussions, and anywhere someone is acting on the project's behalf.
